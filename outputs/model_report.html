Probabilities are too high under drift — use ranks, not risk values

Mean predicted risk: logistic 7.29%, gbm 6.96%; observed 4.26%. Both models sit below the diagonal in every decile. The training period had a higher late rate (5.7% train, 13.0% dev vs 4.3% test). That pattern is consistent with drift; the causal reading is not tested.

Recalibration would need a separate calibration set whose labels are known before scoring. None is fitted here.

What the score leans on

Predictive importance only; a feature here is not a cause of lateness.
